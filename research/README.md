## Key Findings

- **Market Size**: 8.35M+ Filipino international departures/year, ₱350B+ annual expenditure
- **Competitive Gap**: No dedicated Filipino outbound travel companion app exists
- **Core Moat**: Bureau of Immigration readiness + PH passport visa intelligence + PHP budgeting
- **Revenue Model**: Affiliate-first → Freemium subscription → Visa concierge → B2B
- **GTM**: Product-Led SEO + Community seeding → Creator flywheel → Monetization at scale
